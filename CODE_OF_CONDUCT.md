# Code of Conduct

We want a respectful, accessible community for contributors and users of every
background and experience level.

Be considerate, explain disagreements with evidence, accept constructive feedback,
and respect privacy. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention,
doxxing, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable. These expectations
apply to repository discussions, issues, reviews, and spaces where people represent
the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, limit participation, or ban repeat
or severe offenders. Decisions should be proportionate, explained privately where
possible, and consider both impact and context.

For a private conduct report, contact either maintainer through the contact methods
on their GitHub profile: [@omermorad](https://github.com/omermorad) or
[@qballer](https://github.com/qballer). If one is involved, contact the other.
Use GitHub's **Report abuse** feature if a private maintainer contact is unavailable.
Do not publish sensitive evidence in an issue. Reports are handled discreetly;
information is shared only as needed to investigate and respond.
